Amazing Space Facts

🌟 The Sun

The Sun contains 99.86% of all the mass in our entire solar system.

⭐ Light Years

The nearest star to Earth (besides the Sun) is Proxima Centauri, 4.24 light-years away.

🪐 Saturn's Rings

Saturn's rings are made mostly of water ice and rock fragments ranging from tiny to mountain-sized.

🌌 Black Holes

Black holes are regions where gravity is so strong that not even light can escape.

🌙 Moon Distance

The Moon is slowly drifting away from Earth at about 3.8 cm per year.

🪨 Asteroids

The asteroid belt contains millions of rocky objects between Mars and Jupiter.
